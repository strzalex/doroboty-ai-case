# Demo Day checklist

- [ ] Start from the approved `demo_day` release and a fresh deterministic reset.
- [ ] State the initial belief, primary outcome, and why submissions are not the outcome.
- [ ] Demonstrate one candidate flow and one employer decision with synthetic accounts.
- [ ] Trace one stable ID through assignment, exposure, source evidence, generated version, approved version, fit proxy, and downstream result.
- [ ] Show quantitative and qualitative evidence, including at least one counterexample.
- [ ] Name limitations and plausible confounders before the recommendation.
- [ ] Present the 30-day pilot, guardrails, costs, privacy boundary, adoption plan, and decision rule.
- [ ] End with a decision and next test, not a universal claim about AI.
- [ ] Verify the live URL, test accounts, database release, analytics no-op/fail-safe, and rollback deployment.
- [ ] Prepare a recording and static export as fallback; confirm neither exposes credentials or future-stage material.
