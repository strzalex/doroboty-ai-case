# Discovery packet — participant release

This packet is intentionally diagnostic rather than conclusive. It belongs to the
`discovery` release and must not name any later-stage mechanism.

## Candidate interview: C-014

> The job says “strategic impact”, but the form mostly asks about motivation. I did not
> know which project would count as evidence.

The candidate completed one-click apply and later clarified a relevant implementation in
the interview. Their original project was not represented in the application.

## Hiring-manager interview: H-03

> Many answers read correctly, but after reading them I still do not know what the person
> actually did or what changed because of it.

The manager reports higher review volume after one-click apply and no corresponding
increase in useful first conversations.

## Support ticket #1842

A candidate asks where to attach a repository and explain their responsibility, because
the generic text field has no appropriate place.

## Sales note: BrightLabs

The team needs experience deploying alongside finance operations. They shortened this
criterion in the public job post because they feared reducing the application volume.

## Synthetic public review

The application was quick, but the first conversation revealed that the company expected
a different type of experience.

## Plausible diagnoses to test

- one-click apply attracts a different candidate mix;
- the job family or seniority mix changed;
- employers are not ready to review increased volume;
- the public job text does not expose the decision criteria;
- the application asks for motivation instead of concrete evidence;
- language fluency affects how evidence is interpreted;
- the continuation metric is delayed or incomplete.

Participants should triangulate at least three sources and choose one intervention. The
packet must not prescribe the solution.
